Roadmap

The detailed plan, with a checkbox per item, is kept in the development roadmap issue. Nonlinear and multiphysics problems are addressed only once the methods are stable on linear problems.

PhaseContentStatus
0. FoundationsVariables, operators, functionals, common loop, Helmholtz filter, optimality criteria and SiMPL, consistency checks, validation on linear benchmarksDone
1. General constraints and common loopReverse sweep with one adjoint solve per state, general constraints and augmented Lagrangian, frozen regions, interface maps (Step(), Ersatz()), discretisation contract (FixedMesh())Done
1b. Consolidation on linear problemsReaction–diffusion (level set, density, double well), initial hole seeding, benchmark suite with reference values (top88, levelset88, Kim et al. 2020), mesh independence, report of designs, closing 3D linear caseIn progress
1c. Constraints and formulations (linear)GCMMA and field constraints, null-space optimiser, perimeter, stress constraints, minimum and maximum member size, self-weightPlanned
1d. Adaptive meshing (ersatz material)TwoMeshes, then AdaptiveMesh; transfer of the optimiser state at each remeshingPlanned
2a. Nonlinear and coupledDesign-dependent constitutive models, robust forward solve, chains of states and staggered adjoints, global and vector design variables, 3D, multi-material with several level setsPlanned
2b. Formulations on nonlinear problemsPhase 1c constraints on nonlinear and multiphysics problems, design-dependent loads, overhang constraintsPlanned
2c. Path dependence and timeAdjoint over load or time steps with internal variables (viscoelasticity, transient actuation)Planned
3. MPI on fixed meshesDistributed design vectors, filters, optimisers and reaction–diffusionPlanned
4. Body-fitted meshes and cut cellsFittedMesh and CutCell() with the reaction–diffusion level setPlanned
5. Real geometriesSTL/CAD to signed distance, non-design regions, Cartesian background meshes, STL output, body-fitted re-analysisPlanned
6. Instabilities and eigenvaluesBuckling, eigenfrequencies, limit points, connectivity constraintPlanned
7. Octree meshes and multigridAdaptive octree meshes with hanging nodes, geometric multigridPlanned
8. GPUMatrix-free operators and multigrid on GPUPlanned

To be scheduled: level set with topological derivative (Amstutz and Andrä 2006) after Phase 1b, and microstructure design with linear homogenisation.

Research lines: classical (Hamilton–Jacobi) level set, methods as gradient flows, chaining of methods, optimisation under uncertainty.
