Validation

Linear validation of phase 0: compliance minimisation with SIMP (p = 3), a Helmholtz filter and a volume constraint on the physical density, against published values. The scripts are in the folder validation/ of the repository; the fast cases run in the test suite. Measured on an Apple M5 Max, Julia 1.13.0, 4 threads, LUSolver.

MBB beam against top88 with the PDE filter

Andreassen et al., Efficient topology optimization in MATLAB using 88 lines of code, SMO 43 (2011), section 4.2: volume 0.5, E₀ = 1, Emin = 1e-9, ν = 0.3, plane stress, unit square elements, rmin = 0.04 nelx, unit point load at the top-left node, OC with move 0.2, stop at max|Δx| ≤ 0.01. Reference compliances 237.60 (60×20) and 235.36 (150×50). A plain-Julia port of the code (validation/top88.jl) gives 237.5996 in 140 iterations and 235.3632 in 461 (the last compliance the code prints; the tables below give the compliance of the final design).

OC as top88 is OC(adapt = nothing) with StopCriteria(change_tol = 0.01); OC() and SiMPL() use the defaults of HyperShape (adaptive move limits, stop when the objective settles).

  • Cell mean or Gauss points. top88 gives each element one physical density, the cell mean of the filtered nodal field (xPhys = TF'·K⁻¹·TF·x). HyperShape evaluates the filtered field at the Gauss points, so the stiffness varies inside an element: a different formulation, 1.1 % stiffer at 60×20 and 0.3 % at 150×50. The cell mean is obtained by composing CellMean after the filter, CellMean(dΩ) ∘ HelmholtzFilter(dΩ; rmin, ls); with it HyperShape reproduces top88 to four digits.
  • Point load. It is written as the traction dual to the shape function of the corner node on the first top edge, t(x) = −(4 − 6x/h)/h for x < h: its work on a P1 field is the value at the corner.
  • The filter has homogeneous Neumann conditions, as in top88 and SiMPL: the design sticks to the boundary of the domain.

MBB beam against SiMPL

Kim, Lazarov, Surowiec, Keith, A simple and efficient algorithm for topology optimization (SiMPL), arXiv:2411.19421v3. Table 1 (768×256): SiMPL 1.2078e-3 in 50 iterations, OC 1.2234e-3 after 300; table 2 (h = 1/256, 30 iterations): 1.0566e-3. Setup of the authors' code (problem MBB2 of miniapps/simpl in dohyun-cse/mfem): (0, 3)×(0, 1), 768×256, plane strain, ν = 0.3, Emin = 1e-6, volume 0.3, P0 control, body force (0, −1) in the disc of radius 0.05 at (0, 1), roller on the bottom edge for x > 3 − 1/32, stop at the stationarity measure Sₖ = ‖z − P(z − g)‖ ≤ 1e-5 (L2 metric, StopCriteria(stationarity_tol = 1e-5) with LumpedMass(dΩ)).

  • Filter radius. Table 1 was computed with rmin = 0.05: the run_local.sh of dohyun-cse/mfem 28e17f2 (2024-11-25, before the paper) holds the command of table 1 without -fr, and 0.05 was the default of MBB2 until commit c5b9f24 (2025-02-14), which changed it to 0.02. The commit given in the paper (022954b) only runs the mesh study of table 2, with 0.02.
  • Sensitivity of the authors' code. It halves the shear term (topopt.cpp: M = mu*0.5, then 2.0*M*grad:adjgrad), so it is the derivative of λ tr(ε)² + μ ε:ε instead of λ tr(ε)² + 2μ ε:ε: not the exact derivative of their stiffness. mbb(…; sensitivity = :authors) in validation/cases.jl replicates it. With it, HyperShape reproduces the iterates of the authors' log to eight digits (first iterate checked in the test suite at 192×64) and both tables. With the exact derivative the same setup reaches a 0.97 % stiffer design, in more iterations.
  • Iterations. The authors count the initial design as iteration 0, so their 30 iterations of table 2 are 29 steps.
  • OC. The OC value of the paper is taken after 300 iterations without convergence; OC() with adaptive move limits and the default stop reaches −0.99 %.

3D cantilever

(0, 2)×(0, 1)×(0, 1), 24×12×12 hexahedra (12 168 displacement dofs), clamped at x = 0, downward traction on the bottom strip (height h) of the face x = 2 (close to the line load of top3d), Emin = 1e-9, rmin = 2.5h, volume 0.3. No published value: check_consistency passes for P0 and P1, and the design keeps the symmetry in y.
