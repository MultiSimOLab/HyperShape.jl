HyperShape.jl

Work in progress

The package is at the design stage. These pages describe the planned architecture; only a small legacy prototype is implemented today.

HyperShape is a library for shape and topology optimisation of nonlinear multiphysics problems: finite-strain hyperelasticity, electro-, magneto- and thermo-mechanics. It is built on HyperFEM, within the Gridap ecosystem.

Principles

  • HyperFEM is used as a black box. The forward and adjoint problems are HyperFEM computational models that the user builds with their own weak forms.
  • The user configures every solver. HyperShape chooses none: not for the state, the adjoint, the filter or the internal solves of the methods.
  • Analytical derivatives. No automatic differentiation; every sensitivity is verified with a Taylor test.
  • One abstraction for several methods. Density (SIMP), reaction–diffusion and level set share variables, states, functionals and the optimisation loop.
